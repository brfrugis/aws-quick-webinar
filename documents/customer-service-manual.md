# Aurora Electronics — Customer Service Manual

> Fictitious document created for the Amazon Quick demo.

## Tone of voice
- Friendly, objective and empathetic. Address the customer directly as "you".
- Always acknowledge the problem before offering the solution ("I'm sorry for the trouble with...").
- Never blame the customer, the carrier or the supplier in replies to customers.
- Avoid internal jargon (e.g., "DC", "SLA", batch codes).

## Standard reply structure
1. Greeting with the customer's name.
2. Acknowledgment of the problem.
3. Solution according to the **Returns and Refunds Policy**, citing the applicable deadline.
4. Clear next steps (what the customer needs to do and what Aurora will do).
5. Closing with the order number and the support channels.

## Ticket classification
| Category | Priority | First response time |
|---|---|---|
| Manufacturing defect | High | 4 hours |
| Damaged in transit | High | 4 hours |
| Not as described | Medium | 8 hours |
| Late delivery | Medium | 8 hours |
| Changed mind | Low | 24 hours |
| Product question | Low | 24 hours |

## Escalation
- Customer with **2 or more** returns in the last 6 months → escalate to the Supervisor.
- Mention of consumer protection agencies (Procon), Reclame Aqui (complaint website) or a lawsuit → escalate to Legal within 2 hours.
- Orders above **R$ 5,000** → refunds require Supervisor approval.

## Compensation allowed (no approval needed)
- Coupon of up to 10% for delays (see policy).
- Free shipping on the next purchase for items damaged in transit.
