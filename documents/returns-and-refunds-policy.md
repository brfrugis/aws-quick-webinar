# Aurora Electronics — Returns and Refunds Policy

> Fictitious document created for the Amazon Quick demo. Version 3.2 — effective March 1, 2026.

## 1. Right of withdrawal
- Customers may cancel a purchase within **7 calendar days** of receipt (Brazilian Consumer Protection Code, art. 49), for purchases made on the Website, App or Marketplace.
- The product must show no signs of use and be returned in its original packaging with all accessories.
- Return shipping is **free** for the customer; the refund is issued within **10 business days** after the product arrives at the Distribution Center (DC).

## 2. Defective products
- Claim period: **90 days** after receipt (durable goods).
- Up to **30 days**: Aurora replaces the product with a new one at no cost, or issues a full refund, at the customer's choice.
- From **31 to 90 days**: the product is sent to an authorized repair center; maximum repair time is **30 days**. If it is not repaired within that time, the customer chooses between replacement, refund or a proportional price reduction.
- **Exception — Audio line (Pulse Bluetooth Headphones and Boom Speaker):** due to the ongoing quality program (see section 6), defects reported within **90 days** qualify for an **immediate replacement** with a new product, without going through the repair center.

## 3. Product not as described or damaged in transit
- The customer must refuse the delivery or open a ticket within **72 hours** of receipt, with photos.
- Replacement or full refund, at the customer's choice, with shipping paid by Aurora.

## 4. Late delivery
- Delays of more than **5 business days** beyond the promised date entitle the customer to a **10%** coupon on their next purchase.
- If the delay exceeds **15 business days**, the customer may cancel the order with a full refund.

## 5. Refund methods
| Payment method | Refund time |
|---|---|
| Credit card | Up to 2 subsequent statements |
| Pix (instant payment) | Up to 3 business days |
| Bank slip (boleto) | Up to 10 business days, to the bank account provided |
| Aurora store credit | Immediate |

## 6. Quality program — Audio line (internal)
- Since Jan/2026, the Quality team has been monitoring the high return rate of the **Pulse Bluetooth Headphones (P005)**.
- Preliminary root cause: faulty battery batch from supplier *SoundCell* (batches SC-2511 to SC-2603).
- Action: a new supplier has been qualified; return rates are expected to normalize starting with **batch SC-2611**.
- Goal: reduce the P005 return rate to below **6%** by Mar/2027.
