# Lab 07 — Quick Research (intermediate bonus)

⏱️ 10 min to set up + a few minutes of processing · 🎯 Generate a cited report that combines the web and internal data.

## How it works
1. You describe the research goal.
2. Quick proposes a **research plan**, which you can review and adjust.
3. It searches the **web** and the **internal sources** you choose (Spaces, knowledge bases, dashboards).
4. It delivers a **structured report with citations**, which you can refine by chatting.

## Steps
1. Left menu → **Research** → new research.
2. Paste the prompt from [Block 8 in demo-prompts.md](../prompts/demo-prompts.md#block-8--quick-research).
3. Under **sources**, include the `Aurora Customer Service` Space (and the `Aurora Sales` dashboard, if available).
4. Review the proposed plan → **Start**.
5. When it's done, explore the report:
   - Check the **citations** (web vs. internal).
   - Ask for changes: `Turn the recommendations into a table with effort and impact.`

> 💡 **Webinar tip:** start the research **before** the presentation and show the finished result.

## ✅ Validation
- [ ] The report has sections, citations and recommendations.
- [ ] At least one recommendation uses internal data (e.g., Pulse headphones returns, App or Northeast growth).
